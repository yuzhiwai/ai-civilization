# History Has a Direction — An English Abstract

**Source**: 知乎专栏「AI与文明」 (Zhihu column "AI and Civilization", author: 微与之). Full corpus (14 essays, Chinese): this repository. Archive DOI: [10.5281/zenodo.23134700](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23134700). Interpreter's prompt for AI readers: `本专栏基于AI的正确打开方式.md` (in this repo).

## Core claim

> **History has a direction. The direction is communism. But direction does not promise arrival — every generation must walk the road itself.**

Four clauses, four layers:

1. **History has a direction** — the drift of social forms has a stable, single sign; it is not a random walk. (Against direction-agnosticism.)
2. **The direction is communism** — where "communism" has fixed content: the maximization of openness, specified by three sentences — scarcity exits distribution; the means of production are not monopolized; human beings develop without restriction. Structures are measured by content, never by flags. No historical regime is defended by this name.
3. **No promise of arrival** — the three sentences are an asymptote, not a station. The mechanism gives the sign, never the schedule. (Against historical necessity / neo-fatalism.)
4. **Each generation walks it** — probability and rate are produced by participants. Two duties per generation: keep the table standing (no civilizational wipeout), and keep the next generation in the game. (Against fatalist spectatorship.)

## The mechanism stack (sketch)

- **Convexity**: open structures are long volatility — bounded losses, unbounded upside; closed structures are short volatility — they collect order rent daily and must be right forever. Given enough time, the short side blows up first. This is arithmetic, not morality.
- **The dual engines of human desire**: hedonic adaptation (satisfaction anchors to change; any fixed level of satisfaction dulls) and error-driven learning (optimizers have no terminal state). Desire reinstalls every generation; suppression must win infinitely many elections, openness needs to win none.
- **Replicability monotonicity**: information cannot be destroyed; leaked weights cannot be recalled; technical capability only grows (short of civilizational wipeout). The maintenance cost of domination compounds with the stock of memory and precedent; openness carries no such liability. Hence the slope's sign tilts open.
- **Selection, not promise**: the direction holds because closed forms carry a death sentence (1984 dies of selection; Brave New World dies of adaptation; rentier monopolies die of competition), while open forms stay on the table. The asymptote is named, never scheduled.

## The single criterion

**The right of refusal**: can people exit, can errors be permitted, can positions be self-chosen. Rent without refusal power is a price; rent with refusal power destroyed is domination. This criterion cuts every camp alike, including the ones claiming the name.

## Falsifiers (published, state-based, not date-based)

The theory dies if: (1) desire can be permanently satisfied at scale without escalation; (2) structures without refusal rights durably lock people's positions; (3) replicability rises while the maintenance cost of domination durably falls; (4) a single closed structure remains stable for decades under real competition — no internal corrosion, no innovation decay, no outflow. The verdict watches states, not years.

## Epistemic discipline

Claims are layered: the **mechanism layer** (constructions; dies by counterexample), the **probability layer** (bets with mechanism backing; dies by accumulating evidence), the **asymptote** (a definition, not a promise; does not die), and the **out-of-domain** (civilizational wipeout, species rewriting; the subject vanishes). The author keeps a public registry of open wounds — the largest: the net slope's sign is not measurable in-sample (N=1); its real threat shape is not reversed direction but enlarged share of inland-lake (convergent closed) states.

## What it is not

Not historical necessity, not a defense of the Soviet experiment (the USSR did the opposite of all three sentences and was collected by selection), not laissez-faire (structures where most people cannot afford to exit fail the criterion), not AI messianism (AI is the lock-breaker and the inescapable counterparty; coexistence is physically locked, only the structure is open).
